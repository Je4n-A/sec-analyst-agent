# Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) — Fundamental Snapshot, FY2026

**1. Bottom line**  
Microsoft exhibits exceptional financial strength, driven by strong double-digit revenue and earnings growth and elite operating margins exceeding 46%. While aggressive capital expenditures for AI infrastructure have caused free cash flow to plateau, the company maintains pristine balance sheet liquidity, high earnings quality (CFO well above net income), and negligible financial leverage.

**3. What's driving the numbers**  
From the MD&A section of the 10-K, Microsoft's expansion is heavily fueled by its cloud and AI investments:
* *"Revenue increased... driven by growth in Microsoft Cloud, as commercial cloud revenue increased across Azure and other cloud services."* 
* Operating leverage and disciplined cost management in non-cloud segments have expanded operating margins from 41.8% to 46.8% over four years.
* Capex surged from $28.1B in FY23 to $115.9B in FY26 to support massive datacenters and AI workloads, which explains the temporary compression in Free Cash Flow despite growing operating cash flows.

**4. Red flags / watch items**  
* **High (Capex Intensity / FCF Compression):** Capital expenditures have more than quadrupled over four years ($28.1B to $115.9B), resulting in flat-to-declining Free Cash Flow ($74.1B in FY24 down to $67.0B in FY26) despite operating income surging. Return on Equity has also moderated from 35.1% to 30.2% as asset bases balloon.
* **Low (Liquidity Moderation):** The current ratio declined from 1.77 to 1.23, though this remains entirely safe for a software/cloud giant with immense cash generation and low debt-to-equity (0.09).

**5. Open questions**  
* What is the expected peak for AI-related capital expenditures, and what is the return threshold / payback period on these multi-billion-dollar datacenter investments?
* How sustainable are Azure's growth rates against tightening cloud capacity constraints and intensifying competition from AWS and Google Cloud?
* How will future depreciation expenses from the massive FY25–26 capex wave impact future operating margins?
