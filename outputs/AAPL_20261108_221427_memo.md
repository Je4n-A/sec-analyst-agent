# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Fundamental Snapshot, FY2026

**1. Bottom line**  
Apple’s earnings quality improved notably in FY2026 compared to FY2025, driven by a rebound in operating cash flow conversion (CFO/Net Income rising from 0.995 to 1.098) and strong expansion in gross, operating, and net margins. Net income growth (+18.0% YoY) was fully backed by cash generation, with free cash flow surging 37.8% to $136.06 billion.

**3. What's driving the numbers**  
According to Item 7 (Management's Discussion and Analysis), Apple’s top-line and margin expansion was propelled by robust growth across its product and services ecosystem, alongside favorable cost structures:
- **Gross Margin Expansion:** Gross margin expanded to 48.8% in FY2026 (up from 46.9% in FY2025), reflecting favorable mix shifts toward Services and cost efficiencies.
- **Operating Leverage:** Operating income grew to $158.06 billion (a 18.8% increase YoY), outpacing revenue growth and driving operating margins up 100 bps to 33.0%.

**4. Red flags / watch items**  
- **Low (Watch):** None identified in cash quality. CFO/Net Income recovered to 1.10x in FY2026, alleviating temporary working capital pressures seen in FY2025 (0.995x). 

**5. Open questions**  
- How sustainable is the 48.8% gross margin level given component cost inflation and potential shifts in hardware/services revenue mix?
- What drove the substantial increase in accounts payable or other non-cash working capital adjustments that boosted operating cash flow to $145.10 billion in FY2026?
