# AT&T Inc. (T) — Fundamental Snapshot, FY2025

**1. Bottom line**  
AT&T maintains a heavy debt burden with total debt of $143.7B against $126.5B in equity (a 1.14 Debt/Equity ratio), but leverage has gradually improved from 1.32 in 2022. Operating profitability has rebounded strongly following the 2022 restructuring/impairments, providing stable operating cash flow ($40.3B in 2025) and manageable interest coverage (~3.5x). However, persistent below-1.0 current ratios signal ongoing short-term liquidity reliance on credit facilities and debt refinancing.

**3. What's driving the numbers**  
In the 2025 10-K Item 7 (MD&A), management highlights ongoing investments in 5G and fiber networks alongside disciplined capital allocation aimed at debt reduction and balance sheet strengthening. Operating income recovered sharply from 2022's negative results (which were weighed down by large asset impairments and separation adjustments), stabilizing interest coverage above 3.5x in FY2025.

**4. Red flags / watch items**  
- **High Debt Load (High):** Total debt remains above $143B, exposing the company to refinancing risk and interest rate fluctuations, even though Debt/Equity has improved to 1.14.  
- **Compressed Working Capital / Low Current Ratio (Medium):** Current ratios well below 1.0 (0.91 in 2025, down to 0.59 in 2022) indicate that current liabilities exceed current assets, reflecting heavy reliance on revolving credit facilities and ongoing debt rollovers.  
- **Capital Intensity (Low):** Substantial ongoing capital expenditures required for network modernization limit the pace of absolute debt paydown.

**5. Open questions** for a human analyst to investigate next  
- What is the debt maturity schedule over the next 3 years, and what percentage of total debt is floating-rate versus fixed-rate?  
- How do management's net-debt-to-adjusted-EBITDA targets align with planned share repurchases and dividend commitments?
