# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Fundamental Snapshot, FY2026

**1. Bottom line**  
Apple’s earnings quality is robust and stable, showing no signs of deterioration over the last 4 fiscal years (FY2023–FY2026). Cash conversion remains exceptional, with operating cash flow consistently meeting or exceeding net income ($\text{CFO/NI} \ge 1.0\times$), supported by expanding gross and operating margins and massive free cash flow generation exceeding \$98B annually.

**3. What's driving the numbers**  
According to Item 7 MD&A in the FY2026 Form 10-K, Apple’s continued top-line and earnings expansion was driven by widespread growth across hardware and services:  
- *“The Company announces new product, service and software offerings at various times during the year”* — spanning iPhone, Mac, iPad, and wearables alongside expanding Services revenue.  
- Gross margin expansion (reaching 48.8% in FY2026) reflects favorable product mix shifts and cost efficiencies.

**4. Red flags / watch items**  
- **High:** None identified regarding earnings quality. CFO exceeds net income across all years, confirming high cash conversion.
- **Medium:** Working capital shifts (e.g., inventory and accounts receivable fluctuations during major hardware launch cycles).
- **Low:** Moderate rise in capital expenditures ($9.0B to \$12.7B) to support infrastructure and manufacturing investments, though easily covered by operating cash flows.

**5. Open questions for further analysis**  
- How sustainable is the 48.8% gross margin level given component cost headwinds and pricing pressures in global smartphone markets?  
- What proportion of future operating cash flow will be allocated to share repurchases versus AI-related R&D and infrastructure investments?
