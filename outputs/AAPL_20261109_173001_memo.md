# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Fundamental Snapshot, FY2026

**1. Bottom line**  
Apple exhibits exceptional, cash-backed earnings quality with operating cash flow consistently exceeding net income across all four fiscal years. Driven by robust top-line acceleration in FY2026 (+15.1% YoY) and expanding gross margins, Apple generated $136.1B in free cash flow, translating to strong structural profitability and robust balance sheet health despite high share repurchase activity.

**3. What's driving the numbers**  
* **Revenue & Margin Expansion:** According to Item 7 MD&A, Apple's top-line growth in FY2026 was propelled by strength across hardware innovation cycles (including iPhone, iPad, and Mac refreshes) alongside continued expansion in the Services segment. Gross margin expanded to 48.79% due to favorable product mix and operational efficiencies.
* **Cash Conversion:** Operating cash flow reached $145.1B in FY2026, easily surpassing net income of $132.2B (CFO/Net Income ratio of 1.10x), driven by robust non-cash depreciation add-backs and favorable working capital management.

**4. Red flags / watch items**  
* **Low Current Ratio (Low/Medium Risk):** The current ratio hovered near 1.0 (1.15 in FY2026), reflecting Apple's efficient working capital strategy and aggressive deployment of cash into share repurchases and dividends rather than idle liquidity.
* **Declining Book Equity / High Leverage (Low Risk):** Massive share repurchases have historically compressed stockholders' equity, driving reported ROE extremely high (>150%). While structural cash generation mitigates solvency risk, debt levels warrant ongoing monitoring.

**5. Open questions**  
* How sustainable is the FY2026 gross margin expansion (approaching 49%) in light of potential component cost headwinds and competitive pricing pressures in key international markets?
* What is the projected run-rate for capital expenditures relative to cash flows given upcoming investments in AI infrastructure and services?
