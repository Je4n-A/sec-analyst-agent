# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Fundamental Snapshot, FY2026

**1. Bottom line**  
Apple exhibits robust fundamental health, driven by accelerating top-line revenue growth (+15.1% YoY in FY2026), expanding gross and operating margins, and prodigious free cash flow generation ($136.1B). Balance sheet leverage has also substantially improved, with the debt-to-equity ratio dropping from 1.69 in FY2024 to 0.70 in FY2026. Earnings quality remains strong, with operating cash flow consistently covering net income.

**3. What's driving the numbers**  
Growth in FY2026 was propelled by strength across hardware and software ecosystems, highlighted by new product introductions including the 14-inch MacBook Pro, iPad Pro, Apple Vision Pro, and expanded service offerings. According to Item 7 (MD&A), gross margin expansion was supported by a favorable product and service mix as well as supply chain efficiencies.

**4. Red flags / watch items**  
- **Low Liquidity (Working Capital):** Current ratio hovered near parity (0.87 to 1.02 across the period), typical for Apple's cash-efficient working capital model but requiring close monitoring of short-term debt maturities. *(Rank: Low)*
- **CapEx vs. R&D Scaling:** As Apple scales investments in AI and proprietary hardware, capital intensity could pressure free cash flow conversion. *(Rank: Low)*

**5. Open questions for further investigation**  
- How much of the FY2026 margin expansion is structural (services mix) versus cyclical (component pricing)?
- What is the pace of share repurchases and debt retirement contributing to the declining debt-to-equity ratio?
