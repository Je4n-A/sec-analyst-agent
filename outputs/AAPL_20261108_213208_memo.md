# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Fundamental Snapshot, FY2026

**1. Bottom line**  
Apple’s earnings quality shows a mixed picture over the last year (FY2026 vs. FY2025): cash conversion and operating profitability improved with strong cash flow growth outpacing net income growth, but a massive surge in inventory growth relative to revenue creates a near-term working capital headwind. Overall, headline earnings remain backed by robust cash generation, though inventory management warrants close monitoring.

**3. What's driving the numbers**  
* **Margin Expansion:** Gross margin improved to 48.79% in FY2026 (from 46.91% in FY2025), driven by favorable product and services mix. As noted in Item 7 MD&A, the company experienced favorable cost structures and continued growth in high-margin Services revenue.
* **Strong Cash Flow Conversion:** CFO grew 30.15% to $145.10B, outpacing net income growth (18.00%), lifting the CFO-to-net-income ratio from 1.00x to 1.10x.

**4. Red flags / watch items**  
* **Inventory Surge (High):** Inventory jumped 154.3% YoY to $14.54B in FY2026, significantly outpacing revenue growth (15.1%). This ties up working capital and introduces potential obsolescence risk for upcoming product cycles.
* **Receivables Growth (Low):** Accounts receivable grew 10.16% to $43.82B, which is well below revenue growth (15.06%), indicating healthy collection cycles.

**5. Open questions** for a human analyst to investigate next  
* What specific product lines or components drove the $8.8B absolute increase in inventory at year-end FY2026?
* Is the inventory buildup intentional in anticipation of early FY2027 product rollouts, or indicative of softer-than-expected channel sell-through?
