# Apple Inc. (AAPL) — Fundamental Snapshot, FY2026

**1. Bottom line**  
Apple exhibits exceptionally strong earnings quality, characterized by cash conversion well above 100% across multiple years (CFO/Net Income averaging ~1.12 over four years) and robust operating cash flow reaching $145.1B in FY2026. Gross and operating margins have expanded consistently, driven by a higher-margin product and services mix, while capital intensity remains remarkably low (<3% of revenue). However, an aggressive acceleration in inventory buildup in FY2026 warrants monitoring as revenue growth scales.

**3. What's driving the numbers**  
* **Margin Expansion:** Management highlights continuous enhancement in product and services profitability, with gross margins climbing from 44.1% in FY2023 to 48.8% in FY2026. 
* **Top-Line Acceleration:** Revenue growth accelerated significantly in FY2026 to +15.1% YoY (compared to +2.0% in FY24 and +6.4% in FY25), supported by new hardware and software release cycles noted in Item 7 MD&A (*Product, Service and Software Announcements*).
* **Earnings Quality:** Net income tracks closely with operating cash flow, confirming that reported profits are backed by cash collections rather than non-cash accounting accruals.

---

**4. Red flags / watch items**  
* **Inventory Buildup (Medium Risk):** Inventory more than doubled from $5.7B in FY2025 to $14.5B in FY2026, outpacing the 15% revenue growth rate. This warrants attention to ensure it reflects planned product launches rather than slowing sell-through.
* **Receivables Growth (Low Risk):** Accounts receivable grew from $29.5B to $43.8B over the period, but this aligns reasonably well with expanding top-line scale.

---

**5. Open questions for further investigation**  
1. Does the sharp inventory increase at year-end FY2026 represent buffer stock for upcoming holiday/new product rollouts, or is channel inventory backing up?
2. What specific drivers within Services versus Products account for the steady 470-basis-point expansion in gross margins from FY23 to FY26?
